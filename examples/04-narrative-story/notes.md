# Narrative Story Example

## Use Case
3-act video script with storyboard. Best for:
- Brand story videos
- Product launch explainers
- Onboarding sequences
- Documentary shorts

## 4 Narrative Models

| Model | Use Case |
|-------|----------|
| `three-act` | Product launches, brand ads |
| `heros-journey` | Brand stories, "About Us" |
| `cognitive-arc` | Tutorials, science explainers |
| `problem-solution` | Sales videos, product demos |

## Customization

Edit the scaffold.json to change:
- Beat count (default 6)
- Mood (calm/excited/urgent)
- Color theme (auto-mapped from mood)
